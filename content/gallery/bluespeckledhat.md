### Pattern
The [March Hat](https://www.ravelry.com/patterns/library/march-hat-2) by Meghan Babin which is from the free "Year of Hats" collection.

### Materials
* Cascade Yarns 220 Superwash
  * 201m, 100g per skein, worseted weight, 100% superwash merino
  * 1 skein
  * Colorway: 1006
* 4mm & 4.5mm needles

Maybe not the best choice since the pattern is hard to see, but the gift recipient liked it.
