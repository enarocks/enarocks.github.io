### Pattern
[Step by Step Sweater](https://www.ravelry.com/patterns/library/step-by-step-sweater) by Florence Miller in size B, redoing this pattern as I did in [my first sweater](/gallery/stepbystepsweater1/) because I learned that I absolutely hate wearing acrylic.

I feel obligated here to leave a note that fibre preference is personal, and all yarn choices are good if they suit the knitter.

### Materials
* Malabrigo Yarn Rios
  * 192m and 100g per skein, worseted weight, 100% merino
  * 4 skeins (kind of cropped!) for a total of 768m and 400g
  * Colourway: 33 Cereza
* 4.5mm and 5.5mm needles (17 stitches and 23 rows = 10 cm)

I still actually wear this sweater, and I'm super proud of it. I did have to re-knit the sleeves cause I made them too long. I was too lazy to do the italian bind-off and I regret it though, the one I did is kind of sloppy. I always do it now, even if it is tedious... it's worth it.
